# 心象测 Logo 设计说明

## 选定方向
**双圆交叠（vesica piscis）+ 中心金点**  
象征：双象 / 双视角交汇 → 清晰「心象」；金点为洞察之核。

心理品牌调研结论：少即是多；青绿 = 信任与澄明；避免大脑/莲花/十字等陈词滥调。

## 顶级生成提示词（可复用）

```text
Premium production brand logo mark, flat vector, pure white background, NO TEXT.
Two identical perfect circles overlapping horizontally creating a clean vesica piscis (mandorla) in the center.
Left and right crescent segments filled solid deep teal #0F766E.
Center almond is pure white negative space.
Inside the exact center: one tiny solid upright teardrop seed in muted champagne gold #B8952E,
height about 12% of the full mark.
Extremely precise geometry, equal circle radii, perfect symmetry, generous 12% padding.
Swiss / Japanese graphic design restraint, psychology brand trust (Calm / Headspace level).
No gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no paper texture, no ornaments, no brain, no lotus, no medical cross, no purple neon.
Must remain sharp at 16px favicon and 512px hero.
```
