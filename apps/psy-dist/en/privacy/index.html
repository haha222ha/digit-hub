Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-15 · Site: https://psy.xhs365.cn/en/

This Policy aligns with the Waffo Pancake privacy checklist. By using XinXiangCe you acknowledge this Policy. This page is not legal advice.

1. Who we are

XinXiangCe operates the psychological assessment link distribution platform at psy.xhs365.cn. Contact: privacy@xhs365.cn / support@xhs365.cn.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: username, email (if provided), password hash, quota balances.
  • Transaction data: order IDs, amounts, currency, payment channel, fulfillment status. Full card numbers are processed by Waffo Pancake — not stored by us.
  • Usage data: generated links, assessment completion metadata needed to deliver reports.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser/user-agent, Cloudflare country headers for fraud prevention and locale defaults.

3. How we use data

To provide and improve the Service, process payments, prevent abuse, comply with law, and communicate service notices. We do not sell personal data.

4. Legal bases (GDPR / similar)

Where applicable: contract performance, legitimate interests (security, product improvement), consent (optional cookies/marketing), and legal obligations.

5. Sharing

We share data with payment processors (Waffo Pancake; domestic gateways for CNY), hosting/CDN providers, and authorities when required by law. Processors act under contractual safeguards.

6. International transfers

Data may be processed in regions where our servers or processors operate. We apply appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law.

7. Retention

Account and billing records are retained while your account is active and thereafter as needed for legal, tax, and dispute purposes (typically up to several years). You may request deletion subject to legal retention.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, and industry-standard practices. No method of transmission is 100% secure; report incidents to privacy@xhs365.cn. We aim to notify affected users of confirmed breaches within 72 hours where required.

9. Your rights

Depending on your region (GDPR, CCPA, PDPA, etc.), you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, or opt-out of certain processing. Email privacy@xhs365.cn. California residents may request “Do Not Sell/Share” — we do not sell personal information.

10. Cookies

We use essential cookies (session, language preference psy_locale). Analytics cookies, if any, will be disclosed and optional where required.

11. Children

The Service is for users 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes

We may update this Policy and will post the new date on this page. Material changes will be highlighted when practicable.
